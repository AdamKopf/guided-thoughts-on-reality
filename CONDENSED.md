Distinction is the single unavoidable starting point (denying it requires drawing one); possibility, existence, and information are not three separate things in a circle, but this one primitive seen as option, presence, and selection. Selection here is not an act performed by anyone: a possibility simply is this-rather-than-that, and specific possibilities stand in contrast to one another without an external observer or a causal effect on a second system—observer and observed, cause and effect, are themselves distinctions and so cannot come before distinction. Possibilities are local articulations of the absolute, which is not an opposite pole, an emptiness that things are added to, or a source that produces them, but the unmarked baseline prior to selection. Not a completed set, the absolute sits beyond every distinction—including exists/doesn't exist and limited/unlimited: a quality is a contrast, and the whole has nothing outside it to contrast with, so it has no qualities and carries zero information. Nothing inside the absolute is made of a separate substance standing apart from it, yet the absolute is not one pick alongside other picks, just as an ocean is not one wave next to other waves. Plenitude follows by subtraction rather than as an added claim: a limit on the whole would be a line with nothing to draw it, since any limiting rule is itself one local possibility inside the absolute, not a condition on it. With no such line, everything that can be distinguished exists.

Because the absolute selects nothing, distinctions and information belong only to specific possibilities, not to the whole: all information is local (a claim that passes its own test, since stating or denying it is itself a local selection). Globally, "exists" excludes nothing; locally, "real" or "actual" means "here," where foundational selections define coherent spaces—nested regions of shared constraints—and questions about a region can only be asked from perspectives its constraints are able to host. Rival views that restrict reality remain locally true of a region and false only when claimed of the whole. Why a perspective should expect its own region to stay orderly is likewise a local question: the whole weights nothing, so it favors neither order nor chaos, and continued order is found from within rather than guaranteed from above.

Contradictions are not forbidden by an outside law: simultaneously selecting and unselecting the exact same distinction in the same respect leaves no net selection, only an empty pointer—words or symbols that exist locally as patterns while failing to pick anything out. Opposites separated by place or respect remain distinct, a "both" that did pick something out would simply be a further distinct possibility, and negation lives in language rather than in the absolute. Likewise, a local void ("none") is one specific possibility distinct from others, whereas a global void (total nothing, with nothing outside it) is not a second entity but another approximate pointer to the absolute from the "nothing" side, like two names for one referent. Every statement about the absolute, these included, is a local pointer inside it, and misleads less the less it fences the whole onto one side of a line; and while only distinction itself forces universal assent, the rest holds together from within, situating rival views as local patterns rather than claiming to compel anyone who refuses its starting points.
